The Story

How 2025 Played Out

Logan's Redemption Arc

After finishing dead last in 2022 with a brutal −28.1 differential, Logan completed the ultimate revenge tour. Led the league in scoring (1879.96), went undefeated at home (7–0), then demolished Jacob Johnson 138.12 to 85.0 in the final. Toilet bowl to trophy — about that ass tattoo though.

The 188-Point Explosion

Garrett Burton's 3rd Beer Hits The Blake Towers dropped 188.34 in Round 1 — the highest single-game total of the playoffs. The magic ran out in the semis against Blevins, but for one weekend Garrett looked unbeatable.

Cinderella Loses Her Slipper

Eli Hurt's Nobody's Hurt entered as the #7 seed and shocked #2 Gut Check 109.72–100.68. The run ended in the semis but a 3rd-place finish for a 6–8 squad is nothing to scoff at.

Donavon's Meltdown

The #2 seed, after a 10–4 regular season and 1824.56 points, got bounced in Round 1 by the 7-seed. Sources say Donavon immediately Venmo'd the league fees and dipped. The crybaby energy is real — new commish era incoming?

Barnwell's Historic Collapse

I chase brown kids finished on a SEVEN-game losing streak. Despite ranking 5th in points scored (1678.86), the fantasy gods showed no mercy. Sometimes the schedule just hits different.

The Defending Champ Falls

CJ Tackett's Room 40's Nuts crashed to 10th place after winning it all in 2024. The title defense was over before the calendar turned to November. Cycle continues.
