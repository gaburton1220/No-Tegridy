Recap

2025 in Four Acts

The Championship

Logan's Mr Pfizer + Swifties for Kamala dominated the title game, crushing Jacob Johnson's Blevins' RubNTug 138.12 to 85.0. The #3 seed completed a dominant playoff run after leading the league in scoring (1879.96 PF) and going undefeated at home. The 53-point margin was the largest of the entire playoffs.

Cinderella Story

Eli Hurt's Nobody's Hurt entered the playoffs as the #7 seed at 6–8. They pulled off the upset of Round 1, knocking out the #2 seed 109.72 to 100.68. The magic continued to a 3rd-place finish — proof you don't need the best regular season to make noise in January.

The Great Collapse

jacob barnwell's I chase brown kids had the 5th-most points in the league (1678.86) but finished on a brutal seven-game losing streak. The L7 collapse is one for the record books — fantasy football is a cruel, cruel game.

Crybaby Chronicles

Donavon Alderson's Gut Check was the #2 seed at 10–4 with 1824.56 points. A first-round exit at the hands of the 7-seed was too much. Sources say he Venmo'd the league fees and ghosted. New commish era incoming?
