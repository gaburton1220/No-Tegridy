2025 SEASON RECAP

Logan Alderson's Mr Pfizer + Swifties for Kamala dominated the championship game, crushing Jacob Johnson's Blevins' RubNTug 138.12 to 85.0. The #3 seed completed a dominant playoff run after leading the league in scoring (1879.96 PF) and going undefeated at home (7-0-0) during the regular season. The 53-point margin of victory in the finals was the largest of the entire playoffs.
CINDERELLA STORY
Eli Hurt's Nobody's Hurt entered the playoffs as the #7 seed with a 6-8 record. They pulled off the biggest upset of Round 1, knocking out #2 seed Gut Check 109.72 to 100.68. The magical run continued to a 3rd place finish - proving you don't need the best regular season to make noise in the playoffs.
